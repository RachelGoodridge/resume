The Genetic Legacy of a Single Immigrant Behind the Big Bird Hybrid Lineage in Darwin’s Finches

Uses whole-genome sequencing data to assess the genetic legacy of a single immigrant G. conirostris male who founded the reproductively isolated “Big Bird” hybrid lineage on Daphne Major. Despite the lineage’s initial isolation, it has more recently outbred with the resident G. scandens population, with over 70% of scandens individuals now sharing IBD segments with the founder. Individuals carrying more founder ancestry tend to exhibit blunter beaks, revealing the extraordinary and lasting genomic impact of a single immigrant.

A resistance-robust suppression gene drive leveraging the accumulation of recessive deleterious mutations

Proposes a multilocus suppression gene drive that targets multiple distant-site genes, each with only a moderate recessive deleterious effect, to reduce the selective advantage of resistance alleles. As deleterious mutations accumulate across target sites, individuals increasingly inherit them in a homozygous state, imposing a sustained genetic load on the population and maintaining suppression even in the presence of resistance.

Networks of Causal Linkage Between Eigenmodes Characterize Behavioral Dynamics of Caenorhabditis elegans

PLoS Computational Biology

Sept 10, 2021

A collaboration between members of the Scripps Institution of Oceanography and the Section of Ecology, Behavior, and Evolution at UCSD. Applied a dynamical systems approach to reveal high-dimensional, nonlinear interactions in C. elegans behavior. Revealed subtle phenotypic variation between individuals and mutant strains not detectable via traditional methods.

Tracking Changes in Behavioural Dynamics Using Prediction Error

PLoS ONE

May 12, 2021

A collaboration between members of the Scripps Institution of Oceanography and the Section of Ecology, Behavior, and Evolution at UCSD. Presented a data-driven method using empirical dynamic modeling to detect subtle, high-resolution behavioral changes in C. elegans. Compared motion against a reference attractor, enabling individualized and behavior-specific analysis from video-derived time series.
